EUROPA CRM - BENCH SALES AUTOMATION

1. Upload PDF/DOC/DOCX resume in Bench Consultants. Parsed data populates the existing Consultant form and skills field.
2. Create a Job. The Job is visible to all Bench Sales users, while edit/delete remains limited to its creator and Super Admin.
3. Saving the Job creates/synchronizes a linked Requirement.
4. Open the Job record and click View Top Consultant Matches. Normal users see matches from their own consultants; Super Admin sees all.
5. Create a Submission by selecting one Consultant and one Open Job. The system copies all linked details and stores match percentage/details.
6. Change Submission status to Interview Scheduled to create an Interview draft.
7. Complete Interview details and select the consultant to create an Offer draft.
8. Accept the Offer. The system creates an Onboarding Placement, reduces remaining Job vacancies and marks the Job Filled when vacancies reach zero.
9. Use Confirm Joining to record actual start/contract details; it updates the existing onboarding Placement and never duplicates it.

Database note: run all Prisma migrations before using the workflow.
Scheduler note: temporary PostgreSQL outages are logged and skipped instead of terminating the API.
